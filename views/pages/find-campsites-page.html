Coordinator Suggested Campsites

Below is a list of the locations currently suggested by our coordinators. These recommendations are intended to help members find practical and scenic places to use borrowed camping equipment across the UK.

  • Lake District Camp Site – Suggested for hiking and scenic camping. Usually cooler and fresh, with mixed weather and frequent cloud or light rain.
  • Snowdonia Camping Park – Suggested for mountain trips and hiking. Usually cool, windy, and changeable depending on elevation.
  • New Forest Camp Site – Suggested for family camping and cooking. Usually mild with woodland shelter and comfortable outdoor conditions.
  • Peak District Camping – Suggested for walking weekends and outdoor exploration. Usually cool with open hill conditions.
  • Cornwall Coastal Camp – Suggested for coastal camping and family stays. Usually milder with sea breeze and variable coastal weather.
  • Yorkshire Dales Camp – Suggested for countryside camping and longer walks. Usually cool and open with occasional rain.
  • Loch Lomond Camp Area – Suggested for scenic lochside stays and hiking. Usually cool and fresh with wetter conditions.
  • Cairngorms Camp Spot – Suggested for adventurous and experienced outdoor users. Usually colder, windier, and more exposed.
  • Brecon Beacons Camp – Suggested for hiking and nature trips. Usually cool, often cloudy, and changeable.
  • Norfolk Broads Camping – Suggested for calmer waterside stays and family use. Usually mild with open skies and breeze.
  • Jurassic Coast Camp – Suggested for coastal weekends and scenic outdoor trips. Usually mild with windy coastal conditions.
  • Northumberland Camp Site – Suggested for peaceful, remote camping. Usually cool and breezy with open weather.
  • Pembrokeshire Coast Camp – Suggested for walking and family camping near the coast. Usually mild but windy.
  • Exmoor Camp Area – Suggested for countryside stays and relaxed camping. Usually mild to cool with mixed cloud.
  • Forest of Dean Camp – Suggested for woodland family trips and gentle hiking. Usually mild and sheltered.
  • North York Moors Camp – Suggested for scenic moorland trips and walking. Usually cool and breezy.
